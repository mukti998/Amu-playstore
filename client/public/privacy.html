Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 27, 2026

Introduction

AMU STORE ("we", "our", "the app") is a premium Ethiopian furniture retail application operated by Amu Furnishings. This privacy policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and web services.

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address or phone number — used as your login identifier
  • Full name — used for order personalization and display
  • Password — stored securely using bcrypt hashing (we never see or store your plaintext password)

Authentication Data

  • Session tokens stored in secure HTTP-only cookies

Product and Order Data

  • Products you browse, add to cart, or purchase
  • Order information including delivery details

Device and Usage Information

  • Standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, pages accessed)
  • Session duration and navigation patterns within the app

Data Storage and Security

All user accounts, products, and CMS content are stored in Supabase PostgreSQL with encryption at rest and in transit (TLS/SSL). Row Level Security (RLS) policies restrict data access by role.

Passwords are hashed using bcrypt with a salt factor of 10. We cannot recover or view your password.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • Service Providers: Supabase (database and storage hosting)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or valid legal process

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Export your data in a portable format

Account Deletion

You may delete your account by contacting us. Upon deletion, your account information is permanently removed from our database.

Children's Privacy

AMU STORE is not directed to individuals under the age of 18.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the updated policy in the application.
